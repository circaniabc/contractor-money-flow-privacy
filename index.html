Privacy Policy

Contractor Money Flow

Effective date: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Contractor Money Flow is a construction and financial management application designed to help contractors manage projects, expenses, income, labor costs, materials, payments and financial records. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

2. Information Stored in the App

Depending on the features you use, the application may store business details, project information, transaction records, income and expenses, labor and material costs, payment records, account balances, app preferences and security settings.

3. Local Data Storage

The application is designed to store its core project and financial records locally on your device and to support offline use. The developer does not operate a central server for synchronizing these local financial records. Information you voluntarily send to the developer, or information processed by integrated third-party services, is handled separately as described in this policy.

4. Backup and Restore

The app may allow you to export and restore backup files. These files may contain your business, project and financial information. You are responsible for keeping exported backups secure and deleting them when no longer needed.

5. PIN Security

The app may provide a PIN feature to help restrict access. Keep your PIN confidential and protect your device. A PIN is an additional safeguard and cannot guarantee complete protection against unauthorized access.

6. Advertising and Google AdMob

The app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device and app information, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and information used for fraud prevention and measurement.

Such processing is subject to Google's policies and applicable privacy choices.

7. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where applicable, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present consent messages and privacy choices. Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be asked to make or update advertising consent choices. An Ad Privacy Options setting may be available in the app when required.

8. Sharing of Information

The developer does not sell your locally stored project or financial records. Third-party services integrated into the app, including Google AdMob, may process information as described in their own privacy policies. Information you voluntarily provide when contacting support may be used to respond to your request.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Local records generally remain on your device until you delete them, reset app data or uninstall the app. Exported backup files remain wherever you store them until you delete them. Data processed by third-party providers is subject to their own retention and deletion policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Contractor Money Flow is a business-oriented application and is not designed to target children. It is not intended for use by children.

11. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to help protect information handled by the app. However, no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device and backup files.

12. Third-Party Services

The application may use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Their processing of information is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, third-party services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this webpage.
